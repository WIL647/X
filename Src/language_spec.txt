MINI-COMPILER PROJECT: MINI-LANGUAGE SPECIFICATION

* AI Assistance Disclosure: This language specification draft was 
  generated with AI assistance as permitted by course guidelines.

1. OVERVIEW
This document defines the lexical and grammar rules for Phase 1 of the 
Mini-Compiler project.

2. KEYWORDS
The following words are reserved and cannot be used as identifiers:
- int, float, bool, string, if, else, while, return, print

3. OPERATORS & DELIMITERS
- Arithmetic: +, -, *, /
- Assignment: =
- Comparison: ==, !=, <, >, <=, >=
- Delimiters / Punctuation: (, ), {, }, ;, ,

4. IDENTIFIERS & LITERALS
- Identifiers: Must start with a letter or underscore (_).
- Integer/Float/String Literals and single-line comments (//) supported.
